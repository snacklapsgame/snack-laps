Privacy policy

Effective date: November 2026

The short version: Snack Laps does not ask you to create an account. Your personal top 10, unlocked pets, chosen pet and sound setting are saved on your device.

We do not run a server that receives your gameplay or personal information. Google AdMob and Apple Game Center process some information when their features are used.

On iPhone, Google may ask how ads should work before any ad is requested. You can change that choice in the app. The game still runs if you decline, or if the choice cannot be loaded.

What stays on your device

The game uses local storage (localStorage) to remember scores and their dates, unlock progress, your chosen pet and whether sound is muted. These records are not sent to a developer-operated database.

The game has no developer-operated cloud save or account system. Your device or operating system may include app data in a device backup; Snack Laps does not manage those backups.

Ads, device identifiers and tracking

The iPhone app uses Google AdMob to show banner ads and optional rewarded ads. Those ads are requested only after Google's consent tools say advertising is allowed. If the check fails, or you withhold a choice that Google treats as blocking ads, you can still play and the app does not load ads.

Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers, including the advertising identifier (IDFA) when you permit it; ads shown, taps and video views; app interactions; crash and performance information; and an IP address. The IP address is used to infer an approximate location, such as a city or region. That is not GPS and not a precise location. The game does not ask for location permission, does not read your map position, and does not send your on-device score history to AdMob.

That information may be used to deliver and measure advertising, limit how often an ad is shown, prevent fraud, and improve advertising services.

Your consent choices

Where required — usually in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland — Google shows a privacy message before ads. You can allow ads that use your data, choose more limited ads, or refuse consent where those options are offered. Refusing personalization does not always turn every ad off. Google decides whether any ad may be requested. If ads are not allowed, the game continues without them.

On supported iPhones the app can also ask for App Tracking Transparency permission (“Allow” or “Ask App Not to Track”). That system question is separate from Google's message. You can decline and still play. Declining limits tracking and access to the advertising identifier. It does not, by itself, stop every ad or every use of an IP address.

The app shows Google's message first. It asks for tracking permission only when Google's published messages are not already set to show that prompt, and only if you have not already answered it. You should see each question at most once for a decision, not two copies of the same prompt.

How to change your choices

On the iPhone app's start screen or game-over screen, tap Privacy / Ad Settings. When Google says a privacy-options form is required, that button reopens it so you can change or withdraw your ad choices. The app then checks again whether ads are allowed. If they are not, the banner is removed. Changing this does not delete your local scores or pets.

If no extra form is required for your region, the button tells you that and leaves the game as it is. You can still change the iPhone tracking decision in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

The browser preview of the game hides this button and does not use AdMob.

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

Apple Game Center

The app attempts to sign in to Apple Game Center when it opens. If you are signed in, it submits your best score to the Game Center leaderboard. Apple processes the score together with your Game Center identity. Your Game Center nickname and leaderboard score may be visible to other players under Apple's settings.

No separate Snack Laps account is created. Game Center is provided by Apple, and local play does not require successful Game Center sign-in. Manage Game Center in your iPhone Settings. See Game Center & Privacy and Apple's privacy information.

Analytics and support messages

There is no separate developer analytics service in the game, such as Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics or a developer crash-reporting service. AdMob can still process advertising analytics and diagnostics as described above.

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, message and any screenshots. We use it to respond and troubleshoot. Please do not send passwords, payment details or sensitive personal information.

Children

Snack Laps is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly seek personal information from children under 13. A store age rating describes content suitability; it does not turn the app into a child-directed service.

If you believe a child has sent us personal information, a parent or guardian can contact us so we can investigate and delete information under our control where appropriate. Apple and Google manage information they process under their own policies.

Deleting data and your choices

There is no in-game reset button. Deleting the app, rather than offloading it, normally removes its local progress and settings from that installation. This is permanent unless an operating-system backup later restores the data.

To change or withdraw ad consent where Google requires a form, tap Privacy / Ad Settings on the iPhone start screen or game-over screen. Deleting local progress does not remove scores already sent to Game Center or records held by advertising providers. Use Apple and Google's controls, or contact the relevant provider, for that information. Contact us about any support email you want us to delete.

Local game data remains until it is removed or overwritten. Support messages are kept only as long as reasonably needed to respond, resolve the issue and meet applicable obligations. Provider retention periods are governed by their policies. We do not sell a developer-maintained list of players; advertising partners may process data for tracking or advertising under applicable law.

This website
