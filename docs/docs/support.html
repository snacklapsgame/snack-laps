Snack Laps support

Tiny paws. Big adventures. Here are the answers to a few common questions.

How do I play?

Hold the screen to run on the outside track. Let go to hop to the inside track. Munch treats, dodge obstacles and see how long you can keep your run going.

How do I unlock pets?

You start with Pup. Improve your best single-run score to unlock one more pet at each 50-point milestone. There are 101 pets in total, with the last one at 5,000 points. Points are not added together across runs.

Tap PETS on the start or game-over screen to choose any pet you have unlocked. Pets change the look, not the rules.

What changes as I score?

A new obstacle type joins at 50, 100, 150 and 200 points, for five obstacle types in all. A new place appears every 50 points. There are 16 places; after those, the places cycle with new color twists.

Why is there no sound?

Tap the speaker button in the top-right corner to unmute, then turn up your iPhone's media volume. Check whether sound is going to Bluetooth headphones or another speaker.

The iPhone app is set up to play game music even when the silent switch is on. Use the game's speaker button when you want quiet. A browser preview can behave differently and may need a tap before sound starts. The music adds layers as your combo builds.

How do ads and continues work?

A banner may appear at the bottom. After your first crash, you can choose to watch a rewarded ad for one continue in that run. Finish the ad to earn it; then a 3–2–1 countdown gets you ready.

Choose No thanks to finish the run instead. If an ad is unavailable, fails or is closed before the reward is earned, a continue may not be granted. You never have to watch the optional continue ad to start a fresh run. Ads and Game Center need an internet connection.

On iPhone, tap Privacy / Ad Settings on the start screen or the game-over screen to change your ad choice when Google asks for one. The game still runs if you decline or if that form cannot open. The button is not shown in a browser. Details are in the privacy policy.

Where is the leaderboard?

MY TOP 10 is your personal list on this device. Tap Global Leaderboard on the start or game-over screen for Apple's Game Center leaderboard.

Sign in to Game Center in your iPhone Settings and check your connection. The app attempts Game Center sign-in on launch and submits your best score when a run ends. Local play still works if Game Center sign-in fails. Global rankings are available in the iPhone app, not the browser preview.

Can I reset my progress?

There is no reset button in the game. To remove local progress, use Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Snack Laps → Delete App, then reinstall it. Choose Delete App, not Offload App.

This normally removes local scores, unlocked pets and settings from that installation. A device-backup restore may bring app data back. It does not erase scores already posted to Game Center. The developer cannot restore a lost local save.
